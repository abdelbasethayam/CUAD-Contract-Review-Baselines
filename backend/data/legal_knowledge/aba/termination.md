The American Bar Association article identifies term of agreement, suspension
and termination, transition rights, service levels, disaster recovery,
compliance, data ownership, data retention, and data security as important SaaS
contract provisions. The source frames termination as part of the wider
operational and data-risk structure of a contract.

For review support, termination clauses may require closer review when the
contract involves ongoing services or hosted data and the clause does not
address transition rights, data return or retention, suspension effects,
service continuity, or practical notice and cure mechanics.

This system uses the source only as supporting guidance. It does not treat a
termination right as a definitive legal problem.
