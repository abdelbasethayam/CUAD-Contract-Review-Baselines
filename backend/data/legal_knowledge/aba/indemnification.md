The American Bar Association article describes indemnity as a contentious
contract term because it can define liability, impose defense obligations before
fault is finally determined, and create exposure that may be difficult to
estimate without a clear limitation of liability.

For review support, indemnity clauses may require closer review when they use
defend, indemnify, and hold harmless language without explaining the timing and
control of defense obligations; when they are not coordinated with liability
caps; when they impose practically unlimited exposure; or when mutuality and
party roles are not addressed.

This system uses the source only as supporting guidance. It flags potential
review issues based on the uploaded clause text, not because an indemnity
clause exists.
