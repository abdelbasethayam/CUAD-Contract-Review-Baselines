The American Bar Association article discusses the relationship between
limitation of liability provisions and indemnity obligations, especially for
intellectual property claims. The source treats these provisions as connected
risk-allocation terms that should not be reviewed in isolation.

For review support, limitation of liability clauses may require closer review
when the clause is unclear about whether indemnity obligations, defense costs,
settlement costs, IP claims, willful misconduct, or other carve-outs are inside
or outside the liability cap. A potential risk indicator is stronger when the
clause appears internally inconsistent with indemnity obligations elsewhere in
the agreement.

This system uses the source only as supporting guidance. It does not assume a
liability limitation is improper simply because it limits recovery.
