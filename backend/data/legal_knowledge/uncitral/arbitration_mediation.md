UNCITRAL describes its Model Law as a framework covering stages of
international commercial arbitration, including the arbitration agreement,
tribunal composition and jurisdiction, court intervention, and recognition
and enforcement of awards. UNCITRAL also publishes a Model Law on
International Commercial Mediation and International Settlement Agreements.
Contract review may therefore require attention to the selected process,
rules, seat or place, appointment mechanics, confidentiality, and enforcement
language. Model-law material is not a universal rule for every jurisdiction.
