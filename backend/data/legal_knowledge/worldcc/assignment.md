WorldCC identifies assignment and transfer provisions as mechanisms that can
affect control, responsibility, and performance. These clauses may require
review when consent requirements, affiliate transfers, novation, change of
control, or continuing responsibility are not clear. This is a potential
review indicator, not a conclusion that a transfer is invalid.
