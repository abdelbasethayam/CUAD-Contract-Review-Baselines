WorldCC identifies payment, pricing, and charges as commercial allocation
topics. For review support, payment provisions may require closer review when
amounts, invoice timing, due dates, disputed amounts, late-payment consequences,
or suspension consequences are unclear. This guidance does not decide whether
a commercial term is acceptable.
