WorldCC identifies confidentiality and information protection as commercial
contracting topics. For review support, confidentiality clauses may require
closer review when they do not clearly define protected information, permitted
uses, authorized recipients, access controls, or the period for which duties
continue. This is guidance for review context, not a legal rule or binary risk
label.
