WorldCC identifies liability caps and exclusions from liability as a standard
contracting principle area. The source frames these terms as commercial risk
allocation provisions that should be considered in context with the wider deal,
local law, industry regulation, and the parties' agreed allocation of
responsibility.

For review support, limitation of liability clauses may require closer review
when they appear to leave important obligations uncapped, exclude broad
categories of damages without a clear carve-out structure, conflict with
indemnity or warranty obligations, or do not clearly explain whether defense
costs, confidentiality breaches, data incidents, IP claims, fraud, willful
misconduct, or statutory liabilities are inside or outside the cap.

This system uses the source only as supporting guidance. It does not treat the
existence of a liability cap, exclusion, or uncapped carve-out as a legal
violation. It flags a potential risk indicator only when the uploaded clause
itself suggests the allocation may need human review.
