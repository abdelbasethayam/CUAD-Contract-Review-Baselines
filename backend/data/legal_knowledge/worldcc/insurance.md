WorldCC treats insurance allocation as a contract-management topic. Insurance
clauses may require review where the required coverage, policy limits,
certificates, additional-insured status, maintenance period, or relationship
to indemnity obligations is unclear. The system flags only potential review
indicators in the uploaded text.
