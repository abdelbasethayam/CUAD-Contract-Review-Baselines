WorldCC identifies indemnification of third-party claims, including intellectual
property indemnification, as a standard contracting principle area. The source
frames indemnity as a risk-allocation mechanism that should be balanced against
the role of each party, the cause of the claim, mitigation duties, insurance,
liability caps, and the procedures for defense and settlement.

For review support, indemnity clauses may require closer review when they are
one-sided, apply to direct party-to-party disputes without clear drafting, cover
claims not caused by the indemnifying party, omit defense-control or settlement
approval mechanics, lack notice procedures, or are unclear about whether the
indemnity is subject to liability limits.

This system uses the source only as supporting guidance. It does not treat an
indemnity obligation as inherently risky. It flags a potential risk indicator
only when the uploaded clause itself suggests the allocation may need human
review.
