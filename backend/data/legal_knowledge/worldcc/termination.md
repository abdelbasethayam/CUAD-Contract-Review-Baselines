WorldCC identifies term and termination, suspension rights, and termination
assistance as standard contracting principle areas. The source frames these
terms as commercial mechanisms that should be clear, workable, and aligned with
service continuity, transition needs, outstanding payments, and the parties'
operational responsibilities.

For review support, termination clauses may require closer review when they
allow immediate termination without a cure period for curable breaches, create
unclear notice requirements, omit transition or post-termination assistance
where continuity matters, make renewal termination deadlines hard to administer,
or fail to describe the effect of termination on accrued payment and survival
obligations.

This system uses the source only as supporting guidance. It does not treat a
termination right as inherently risky. It flags a potential risk indicator only
when the uploaded clause itself suggests the termination mechanics may need
human review.
