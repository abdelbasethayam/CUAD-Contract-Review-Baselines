WIPO explains that non-disclosure and confidentiality agreements address
non-disclosure and limits on use of information learned by a party. WIPO
notes that an effective NDA should define confidential information, identify
who may access it, describe protective measures, and limit the obligations to
a reasonable period. The system uses this public source as review guidance,
not as a binary risk definition.
