The International Bar Association handbook discusses indemnities as provisions
that may allocate losses connected with breaches of warranties, covenants, or
specific compliance obligations. The discussion emphasizes that the appropriate
scope depends on the party role, the risks involved, and the contractual
context.

For review support, indemnity clauses may require closer review when the clause
does not clearly tie indemnified losses to defined breaches, conduct, or
third-party claims; when it allocates broad losses without explaining whose acts
or omissions trigger responsibility; or when the duty to defend, settlement
control, and notice obligations are unclear.

This system uses the source only as supporting guidance. It does not convert
the IBA material into jurisdiction-specific legal rules.
