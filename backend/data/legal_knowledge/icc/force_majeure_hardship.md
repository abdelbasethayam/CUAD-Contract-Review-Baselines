ICC explains that force majeure and hardship clauses address unforeseen events
that prevent or impede performance or make performance excessively onerous.
The public ICC overview describes listed events plus a general formula for
force majeure, and hardship options involving renegotiation, adaptation, or
termination. For review support, the clause text should be checked for event
scope, notice, mitigation or overcoming consequences, and the consequences of
temporary or continuing impediment. This is not a jurisdiction-specific rule.
