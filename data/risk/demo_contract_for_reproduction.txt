Customer shall pay the annual subscription fee stated in each Order Form. Customer commits to a minimum of 100,000 transactions per contract year. Fees paid or payable are non-refundable, including where the service is unavailable for an extended period.

Except for payment obligations, confidentiality breaches, and indemnification obligations, neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages. In no event shall Customer's aggregate liability exceed ten times the fees paid or payable during the Term. Supplier's aggregate liability shall not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the previous one month.

Customer shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Supplier and its affiliates from any and all claims, losses, damages, liabilities, and expenses arising out of Customer's use of the Services. Supplier shall have no obligation to indemnify Customer for third-party intellectual property infringement claims.

If Customer terminates for convenience, Customer remains liable for all fees for the entire remaining term and all minimum commitments.

7. INSURANCE
Supplier shall maintain commercially reasonable insurance coverage during the Term and provide certificates upon request. No minimum policy limits are stated in this Agreement.

8. ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL
Customer may not assign this Agreement without Supplier's prior written consent, which may be withheld in Supplier's sole discretion. A change of control of Customer is deemed an assignment. Supplier may assign this Agreement to any affiliate without consent.

9. INCORPORATED DOCUMENTS AND PRECEDENCE
The Services are also subject to Supplier's online terms and the policies referenced on Supplier's website, each of which may be updated from time to time. If an Order Form conflicts with this Agreement or online terms, Supplier's online terms prevail. No fixed version of the online terms is attached to this demonstration fixture.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES
This Agreement is governed by the laws specified in the applicable Order Form. Each Order Form may designate a different forum and dispute procedure.

11. POST-TERMINATION EFFECTS
Upon termination, Supplier may disable Customer access immediately. Supplier will provide transition assistance only at its then-current rates and only if it chooses to do so. Customer must export all data before the effective termination date.
