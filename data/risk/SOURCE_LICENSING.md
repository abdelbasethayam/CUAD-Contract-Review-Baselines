| Source family | Expected tier | Usage | License status |
|---|---|---|---|

| Commercial/SaaS market-practice notes | B | source-specific permission required | VERIFY_SOURCE |
| General doctrine notes | B | source-specific permission required | VERIFY_SOURCE |

Do not commit book chapters, subscription-only pages, or proprietary checklist text unless the project has explicit redistribution rights.
The imported team playbook is a policy artifact; its source references do not automatically grant the project a license to reproduce the underlying works.
